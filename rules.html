Single-hand stages

Stage 1 and Stage 24 specify weak hand only. Stage 8 and Stage 16 specify strong hand only. Rule 10.2.8 is the key procedural rule.

Holding a magazine in the opposite hand is not, by itself, described as the prohibited act. The critical risk is touching or scooping the handgun with the other hand, except for the listed exceptions such as reloading or clearing a malfunction.

Hard cover target review

Use this lookup whenever a paper target is partially hidden, cut, painted, taped, or obscured by a prop or hard cover surface. Check whether every scoring zone remains partly visible and whether the A-zone is completely hidden.

Moving target review

Stages with activated targets should be checked for activator identity, visible-at-rest status, disappearing target status, reset consistency, and range equipment failure handling.

Before walkthrough

  • Confirm the written stage briefing controls over published diagrams.
  • Identify all scoring targets, no-shoots, poppers, plates and activators.
  • Note whether any target remains visible at rest or disappears after activation.
  • Confirm firearm ready condition where the published stage text is blank.

During walkthrough

  • Check fault lines, shortcuts, ports and barriers from each planned view.
  • Check minimum distance to metal targets and metal hard cover.
  • For single-hand stages, plan how to pick up the gun, reload, clear malfunctions and avoid touching the handgun with the other hand outside the exceptions.
  • For hard cover targets, inspect scoring-zone visibility and no-shoot overlap.

Ask the RO / CRO if unclear

  • Does this target remain visible at rest or count as disappearing?
  • Where exactly does strong/weak hand only begin?
  • Is this object hard cover, soft cover, a barrier, or a stage prop?
  • Is this start position demonstrated with heels, hands, body, or object contact?
